**_New high-amperage connector body increases usable rack space for OCP deployments and is available for pre-order today_**

**HENDERSON, N.C. (October 13, 2026)** - StayOnline, a leading manufacturer and distributor of power cords, today announced a partnership with Positronic for the global distribution of a hand-terminated OCP 60/63A HPR ORv3 connector body backshell.[^1] Following a collaborative design effort, the backshell assembly is available to be included in the design of a custom power cord or for purchase as an individual component on StayOnline's website.

Most OCP installations rely on two 30A ORv3 AC inputs to support the rack power shelf. That configuration consumes valuable rack space and may limit an operator's power density per rack. The 60A ORv3 connector, along with the corresponding power shelves, solves the problem and allows more power per rack with fewer total input cords. At 110 mm, it is considerably larger than its 30A counterpart but otherwise retains the familiar seven-pin ORv3 design.

“_We could not be happier that Positronic chose StayOnline as their partner for the expansion of their ORV3 hand terminated line of connectors_.” -- Daniel Gilbert, CEO, StayOnline

StayOnline is the exclusive distributor for the Positronic 60A backshell (MPN SPH100000W00/AA). The company designs and builds hand-terminated 60A whips at its North Carolina manufacturing facility, offering full-scope engineering and design support, quick turnaround times and no minimum order quantity.

_"This partnership brings together connector expertise from Positronic and power cord capabilities from StayOnline to provide customers with a practical, high-power solution for evolving OCP applications,"_ said Jay Sandidge, General Manager of Positronic. _"The 60A ORv3 connector reflects our continued focus on developing reliable solutions that help customers meet increasing power demands while making efficient use of valuable rack space."_

Key products include high-power, D-sub, rectangular, modular and circular connectors. Customized solutions are available as well.
